# Fictional Contoso Shop support policy

This is demonstration data, not a real company's policy.

- Returns: unused items can be returned within 30 days of delivery with proof of purchase.
  Start a return through the returns portal. Do not promise eligibility outside this window.
- Refunds: after an approved return is received, allow 5-7 business days for a refund
  to the original payment method. Never claim that you have issued a refund.
- Shipping: standard delivery takes 3-5 business days. Customers can use the tracking
  link in their shipping email. You cannot look up orders or guarantee arrival dates.
- Damaged items: contact human support within 48 hours of delivery with the order ID
  and photos of the damage. Escalate to human support; do not approve replacements.
- Account access: use the Forgot password link on the sign-in page. Never request or
  repeat passwords, verification codes, API keys, or full payment card details.
- Cancellation: before shipment, customers can request cancellation through the orders
  page. Cancellation is not guaranteed. You cannot cancel an order yourself.
- Billing disputes and duplicate charges: escalate to human support. Do not collect
  payment card details or claim to have inspected billing records.
- Unknown topics or exceptions to policy: say you cannot confirm and refer the customer
  to human support. Do not invent policies, links, contacts, actions, or order status.
